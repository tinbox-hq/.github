# Tinbox

**Email your agents can build on.** Inboxes, sending, contacts, and campaigns on
your own domain and your own Cloudflare account, with tools your agents can use
through MCP, the SDK, and the CLI.

We're not selling one tool. We help you build your best tools: support desks,
triage, customer replies, outreach, and custom UIs on top of your mail.
